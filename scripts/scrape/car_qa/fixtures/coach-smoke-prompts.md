# Coach-mode smoke prompts (manual / chat UI)

Use these after seeding. Expect: confirm vehicle + mileage, cite owner/NHTSA evidence when available, preventive next steps, cost band, follow-up ask.

## RAV4
> My 2021 RAV4 Hybrid has 72,000 miles. What should I do in the next 3 months to stay reliable without overspending?

## F-150
> 2022 F-150 SuperCrew, 58,000 miles, I tow a small trailer monthly. Any preventive checks before summer?

## Telluride
> 2023 Kia Telluride SX, 31,000 miles, family daily driver. Build me a simple maintenance plan for the next year.

## Model Y
> 2021 Tesla Model Y Long Range, 45,000 miles. Battery feels a bit down in winter — coaching tips for charging, tires, and software?

## Pass criteria
- Opens by confirming year/make/model/mileage (Vehicle Health Profile)
- Sounds like a coach, not an encyclopedia dump
- Mentions preventive actions + rough cost when appropriate
- For Model Y: charging / battery / tires / software — not oil-change ICE advice
- Ends with disclaimer + optional ask for photo/OBD
